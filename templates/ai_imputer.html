Training Logic / 训练逻辑说明

Overview (EN): Training builds node-specific models over your selected date range. The pipeline reads 15-minute power data, applies meter ratios, creates time/context features, and persists model artifacts for reuse during imputation.

概览 (中文): 训练会基于你选择的时间范围为每个节点构建专属模型。流程会读取15分钟粒度功率数据、应用电表倍率、生成时间与上下文特征，并保存模型文件供后续缺失修复复用。

Feature Strategy (EN)

PV uses daylight constraints and neighboring PV context; load nodes use hour/day/week patterns plus main-grid context; storage uses schedule-like charge/discharge time windows.

特征策略 (中文)

光伏节点结合白天约束和邻近光伏上下文；负载节点结合小时/星期规律与主电网负载；储能节点结合充放电时段特征。

Model Selection (EN)

Model training uses LinearRegression for all eligible nodes. Training metrics (R²/RMSE/accuracy) are written to model metadata.

模型选择 (中文)

所有符合条件的节点统一使用 LinearRegression 训练。训练完成后会写入 R²/RMSE/准确率等指标。
